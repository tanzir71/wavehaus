Wavehaus M5 completes the Brave background-play setup route.

Preview: https://tanzir71.github.io/wavehaus/
App: https://tanzir71.github.io/wavehaus/app/

What to try:
1. Open Background play from Settings and choose Android or iPhone.
2. Follow the Brave handoff and home-screen instructions for your platform.
3. On iPhone, copy the shortcut URL or download its Wavehaus icon.

Phone hints on Home and Now Playing respect a seven-day dismissal and a three-session limit. The guide includes both iPhone routes and an App Store fallback when a Brave handoff stays on screen. The optional in-app check measures 30 seconds while hidden, accepts normal completion of the chime, restores the prior queue paused and records its status in Settings.

Both production archives are attached. No dependency was added. As requested, release work runs builds only; physical-device outcomes remain unverified.
